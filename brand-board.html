05 · Rules of the house

DO
Keep pages ivory with generous whitespace — flour is the hero. Let product photography and the gold hairline carry the warmth.
DON'T
No pure #FFFFFF backgrounds, no saturated greens/reds from a generic UI kit, no gold-gradient text on body copy, no rounded-2xl pill-everything playfulness.
DO
Use tabular numerals everywhere money or nutrition appears — columns must align or the transparency promise reads as sloppy.
DO
Reserve the metallic gradient for the monogram, the footer rule and the Lab certificate edge. One jewel per screen.
